## Shared rules

- Business timezone: `Asia/Aden`.
- Timestamp filters use an inclusive start and exclusive end: `>= start_at` and `< end_at`. Date-only fields use the equivalent local calendar dates.
- Monetary results remain decimal strings and are grouped by their transaction `currency_id`. Currencies are never added together unlabeled. Existing historical base amounts may be shown separately but do not replace transaction-currency totals.
- Count and quantity metrics have no currency. Loyalty points are unsigned integers.
- Reporting is read-only and uses the Sprint 0–17 facts below. It does not create a second source of truth.
- Permission names in this document are planned Sprint 18 permissions; they were not registered at the baseline.

## Measures that must not be conflated

| Measure | Meaning and source |
|---|---|
| Order value | A commercial snapshot (`orders.subtotal`, `discount_amount`, and `total`) recognized by this report when an Order is confirmed and not cancelled. It is not a delivery, receipt, or GL revenue figure. |
| Delivered orders | A logistics outcome counted from current Order/Delivery lifecycle facts and `deliveries.delivered_at`. Delivery does not by itself prove GL revenue or treasury receipt. |
| Accounting revenue | Revenue-account General Ledger movement from posted/reversed Journal entries, measured by `journal_entries.entry_date` and `journal_entry_lines`; it is not derived by renaming Order totals. |
| Collected COD | The operational amount recorded in `deliveries.cod_collected_amount` for a delivered Delivery. Until posted settlement, it remains attributable to the driver rather than company treasury. |
| Treasury receipts | Posted General Ledger movement into a cashbox, bank account, or company Electronic Wallet treasury GL account. It is not inferred from delivered status or collected COD. |

## Scope decision

All metrics defined in this contract can be derived read-only from the 39 applied Sprint 0–17 migrations. Sprint 18 needs query code, authorization, presentation, tests, and streamed CSV only; it does not need a reporting summary table, mutable KPI balance, inventory valuation table, or another ledger.
